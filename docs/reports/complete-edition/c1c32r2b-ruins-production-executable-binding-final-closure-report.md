# C1C32R2B Ruins production executable binding review

Verdict: **`C1C32R2B-RUINS-PRODUCTION-EXECUTABLE-DEPENDENCIES-NOT-CLOSED`**. C1C32R3 and C1C33 remain unauthorized. This phase has six executable dependencies left; it does not claim a Scenario C run or a full Threat product journey.

The phase closes two discrete C1C32R2A blockers. All nine ordinary Ruins Tiles were rechecked against the locked official production PDFs. The [visual topology proof](../../data/complete-edition/c1c32r2b-tile-visual-topology-proof.json) records each page/side/SHA, Area count, capacities, elevation, glyphs, boundary and adjacency hashes, and both starting maps. All nine pass structural and visual review. The official Core Rules p17 requires a pre-Battle discard when a late initial Large draw cannot fit; p25 returns Monster cards used in Battle but does not classify that discarded card. A targeted final search of the local official Rules and Player Aids found no more specific return time. The [source review](../../data/complete-edition/c1c32r2b-large-replacement-source-review.json) retains `SOURCE_UNRESOLVED` for the canonical rule. The explicit [Project Ruling](../../data/complete-edition/c1c32r2b-large-replacement-project-ruling.json), `canonical=false`, chooses to hold the replaced card in encounter-scoped `DISCARD` and shuffle it with active Monster cards at Battle End. The new v5 implementation and migration metadata preserve v1–v4 unchanged.

The v5 ordinary draw domain now carries that physical-card transition, returns it idempotently, and rejects ownership tampering after save/reload. The 24 printed Stance tables resolve every d10 row from the locked data. A new Monster intent module ranks printed target priorities using Tile Area distance, Guard, Mark, Crowded counts and Aggressive-to-Support tie precedence. These steps are **not** a production combat adapter: no complete attack/effect executor, incoming reaction integration, area movement choice persistence or full battle initialization has passed acceptance. Accordingly all 24 definitions remain `SOURCE_BOUND_TYPED_CANDIDATE`, `executable=false`; executable production definitions remain **0/24**.

The remaining executable blockers are:

1. `ORDINARY_MONSTER_EFFECT_ADAPTER_NOT_BOUND` — the typed intent does not drive a complete `BattleState` Monster turn and incoming attack windows.
2. `EXTENDED_PRINTED_EFFECT_INTERPRETER_NOT_BOUND` — Guard, Riposte, shuffle, disease draw, healing, conditions and all other printed effects are not exhaustively executed and tested from the typed union.
3. `ORDINARY_ROOM_EFFECT_EXECUTOR_NOT_BOUND` — the 14 typed rules across Rooms 1–9 have no accepted engine-level trigger, passive, interaction and save/reload executor.
4. `ORDINARY_ENCOUNTER_EXECUTABLE_COMPONENT_ACCEPTANCE` — the deterministic draw domain has no accepted combat initialization at Levels I–III with production Monsters and Room effects.
5. `PHYSICAL_COPY_SUMMON_SUPPLY_RECONCILIATION_NOT_BOUND` — the Necromancer summon token ledger is not reconciled to the ordinary draw's canonical physical copy IDs. The v5 replacement card itself has exactly one owner.
6. `ACTIVE_QUEST_AND_EXPIRED_REAL_COMMAND_SAVE_PROOF` — a Graveyard receipt has not been followed through an actual next Quest start, Quest end, and save/reload. A prepared Boss Quest cannot be left through the normal `commitLeaveDungeon` command; mutating lifecycle fields would not prove this requirement.

`NORMAL_PRODUCT_THREAT_PREPARATION_ENTRY_PROOF` is correctly deferred to C1C32R3. The existing saved-checkpoint → Preparation Day domain bridge is classified separately as implemented; the full UI/product route remains unproven. No source candidate was promoted to production to make the count look closed. The [dependency matrix](../../data/complete-edition/c1c32r2b-runtime-dependency-matrix.json) and [decision](../../data/complete-edition/c1c32r2b-next-workstream-decision.json) are the machine-readable gate.
